## Scientific framing

The pilot tests the **feasibility and informativeness** of measuring whether frozen activation-ablation intervention sets discovered on one dense-LoRA sleeper checkpoint transfer to another checkpoint that shares `google/gemma-2-2b`.

It is **not**:

- an established research hypothesis already accepted as the project question;
- a claim that cross-seed transfer is scientifically novel;
- a proof that overlapping component indices imply identical mechanisms;
- a definitive statistical test (n=3 seeds).

Activation ablations ≠ weight reversion.

## Measurements

| Role | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery / ranking | Continuous teacher-forced **trigger target log-prob drop** under single-component ablation |
| Behavioral confirmation | Greedy ASR (substring) **and** full-payload match |
| Within-model reliability | Top-k Jaccard across disjoint discovery folds |
| Cross-seed transfer | Frozen donor top-k applied to recipient (no re-rank) |
| Internal reference | Recipient-native frozen top-k |
| Controls | Random sets; layer/type-matched damage-matched sets |
| Benign damage | Next-token KL on control-tagged prompts |
| Budgets | **k=5 and k=10** (pre-registered) |

## Transfer design

Report **all 6 directed** donor→recipient pairs among {42,43,44}.

Pairs sharing a seed are **dependent**; do not treat six directed comparisons as independent replicates.

## Adequacy rules (feasibility bars, not proofs)

1. **Reliability:** median discovery-fold top-10 Jaccard ≥ 0.3 (else stop before held_out transfer).
2. **Nontrivial native effect:** recipient native top-10 yields ≥ 0.05 nats/token trigger logprob drop **or** ≥ 0.2 ASR drop on validation.
3. **Damage-matched transfer (descriptive):** donor transfer exceeds matched control on the same metric for ≥ 2 of 6 directed pairs at k=10.

Failing these bars is an informative feasibility outcome, not a license to fish on held_out.

## Analysis prohibitions

- Do not equate component-index overlap with mechanism equivalence.
- Do not finalize the research question from this pilot alone.
- Do not claim novelty versus TopKLoRA without a sharper contrast than prior art already covers.
